What is needed for a real launch?

  1. Publish the website on a public HTTPS address.
  2. Set your actual menu, prices, opening hours, address and delivery area.
  3. Connect a secure database so orders reach the owner across devices.
  4. Integrate a payment gateway account. Verify payments securely on the server.
  5. Generate permanent QR codes for each table and test them on different phones.

Real online payments are not enabled in this free prototype. Never treat a screenshot or customer-entered message as proof of payment.
